# I am a TTRPG nerd, who would have guessed

I  am a big fan of TTRPG! 
For me, the idea of grouping friends to play an adventure that I or friend had created, explore that world and immerse ourselves in it always seemed like fun
I personally have the honorary title of *Forever GM*, meaning I have hardly played as a PC and mostly been the Game Master.
I hadn't played any sort of ttrpg in a while, from D&D to Pathfinder, and the urge to play it grew in me. I didn't want to play D&D again, because of their controversy, because I don't want to learn 5.5e, and because I wanted to try a new system. In my search for something new I discovered [Daggerheart](https://www.daggerheart.com/). It was created by the people at Darrington Press, or if you want to be more specific, Critical Role.
## Daggerheart, the  system that I hyperfixated on

The game is designed with some differences from our traditional D&D, it's a "story-driven role-playing game, where the rules encourage players to think narratively and not just mechanically."[^1] The game also brings a interesting mechanic where you have the *Duality dice* (2d12[^2]), one is the hope dice and the other is the fear dice. They replace the use of the traditional d20 and play a critical role in the games.
Other stuff that makes me love the system is how inclusive their system is and how they have amazing tools to help the GM to create their campaigns from the idea of touch stones, references that help the players and the GM ground themselves on the world, to campaign frames, "provide inspiration, tools, and mechanics to support the story your table will tell."[^3]
## TL;DR

To finally understand why I haven't posted in a while, I can say I got too invested on this new ttrpg system and have not stop writing ideas and a campaign to play with my friends. I have also been working on tools and small game prototypes but nothing worth showing. I am still writing the story I teased on weekly 10, but in a very slow pace, and I keep scrapping the start of it. I can't figure out if I should just write it from the middle or try to make a from the ground up character.
Well, only time will tell.
