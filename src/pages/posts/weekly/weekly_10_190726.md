## Lets start with the Book I am reading

Right now, yours truly, is reading [Aurora Burning](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/40516960-aurora-burning), a sci-fi book that follows the story of squad 312, its brings really interesting concepts on sci-fi that I plan to write about in a more in depth post in the future.
