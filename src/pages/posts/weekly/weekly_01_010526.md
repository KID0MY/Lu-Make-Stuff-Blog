## What are these "weeklies"???

This is a new format I’m trying out, a short weekly page to help me get used to writing at least a little bit every week. These won't be heavily edited or filled with pictures; instead, they’re just a place for me to write down interesting things I read, found, or did during the week.

## Future proofing the website

I finally "future-proofed" the webiste a little bit👌I’m adding four tabs to my posts page to organize my writing into neater spaces:

- **Weekly:** Where I’ll write these small weekly updates.

- **Rambles:** These will follow my old format, taking the time to share a project, a passion, or an opinion.

- **Tutorials:** Exactly what it sounds like!

- **Stories:** I’ve always been fascinated by storytelling, so this is where I’ll post chapters of the stories I write :D
