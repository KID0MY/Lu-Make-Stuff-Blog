# Witch Hat Atelier is Peak!

Finally, after years of waiting this amazing manga has become an anime! I started reading it **around 2019** and always dreamed of how it would look like animated, and thanks to **Studio Bug Films** it's now a reality! The way that they have portrayed the characters and scenes is spectacular and not only they haven't *(so far)* removed anything from the manga they have actually **added new scenes**! This is such a vibrant and captivating show, I love how each character has a unique voice and how they don't have all an average American accent, like my boy Tartah who is **fully Scottish**!![Witch Hat Atelier TV Series 2025–   IMDb](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/M/MV5BZjQ2NGVmNzEtODg3Mi00MmViLTkzNGYtNWExNGEzNmNkOGYwXkEyXkFqcGc@._V1_FMjpg_UX1000_.jpg)

I couldn't forget to mention the amazing soundtrack that they are putting together for it! They have [Yuka Kitamura](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm6517135/) as the Musical Composer, for those who dont know she worked on projects like Sekiro, Darksouls 3 and Elden Ring!

# Project Hail Mary

"amaze, amaze, amaze" - Rocky

What a movie! I recently watch it with friends and **wow**, it's beautiful! Ryan Gosling acting is great and the whole story of him as a funny scientist stuck in a spaceship with his friend alien rock in a journey to save their world is so poetic as it is funny. It's a mixture of comedy with Drama where we both laugh with and feel bad for the main hero of the story as we understand their struggles. ( I Personally cried while watching it).

Knowing that a lot of scenes were shot practically and effects like the Astrophage cloud scene was filmed by "hacking" an [Alexa LF Camera](https://www.arri.com/en/camera-systems/cameras/alexa-lf) (They just removed the infrared sensor from the camera) is so interesting.
