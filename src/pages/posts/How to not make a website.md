"And so she begins, to touch the computer…" - <cite>Halimede</cite>

I was always a big computer nerd and never really shy away from it, I was always amazed how people made things and how I could also do the same. After a while i grew and learned how to use computer to code and make my own games, work on back-end and make simple applications. But i never, **never** adventured myself on making front-end. Its not that doing artsy stuff scares me, I am a graphic designer by passion nonetheless,  but having to "code" my front end always made me take a step back and return to my tradition ways. BUT NOT TODAY! TODAY I MAKE MY WEBSITE!

## Act I: Why?

### **The short answer:**

- Because I want, lol.

### **The long answer:**

- I have always been fascinated by blogging and the idea of writing from stories to projects so that people could read about it. I unfortunately, from a young age, I made the connection that writing and reading, weren't pleasurable things but chores, and it has severely affected me on my adulthood. now I am trying to start this so I can try to finally spark and re-make this connections on my brain.

## Act II: The inspiration

I recently came across the Website of [Ari](https://adryd.com/), a pretty cool nerd that sparked my interest in blogging again. From there my whole crazy with writting cameback.

I started to go after other website to read and get inspirations, and along the way learned a lot of interesting stuff!
