## What is C++?

C++ is a programming language created by Bjarne Stroustrup and his team at Bell Laboratories in 1979. Forty years later, it is one of the most widely used languages in the world; we can find C++ applications everywhere, from the bottom of the oceans to the surface of Mars.

As the name implies, <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">C++ was derived from the C language;</mark> Bjarne’s goal was to add <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">object-oriented programming</mark> into C, a language well-respected for its portability and low-level functionality.

> [!info]- What is Object-oriented programing?
> Object oriented programming, also know as OOP, isa computer programming model that organizes software design around data, or objects, rather than functions and logic.

## Why learn C++? 
Among many other things:
- It is <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">fast</mark> and <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">flexible</mark>.
- It is <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">well-supported</mark>.
- It forces you to think in new and creative ways.

>[!warning] A quirk
C++ is a case-sensitive language. Case sensitivity means that your keywords and variable declarations must match the case.
